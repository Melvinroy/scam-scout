# Navigation

The bar should make Scam Scout feel like a product with a map, not a single landing page with four links.

## References

- [Nebius](https://nebius.com). Observed in the 21 Oct 2026 capture: a sticky light bar, a bold square logo badge, text menus (Products, Solutions, Why Nebius, Developers, Pricing, Company), and pill actions at the right. The owner’s brief for the menus themselves: a frosted light panel, items grouped under headings, each item an icon tile, a title and one line. That is the structure we are matching, not their menu labels.
- [RibbonPay](https://ribbonpay.example). The menu is the site map. Descriptions stop you opening the wrong page.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com). Product items are concrete, and one button is the primary action.
- [Linecraft](https://linecraft.example). The bar stays short. The button is the product.
- [MinnowRisk](https://www.minnowrisk.example). A risk site splits product, use cases and trust. We only include pages this project actually has.

## Our menu

| Menu | Groups | Pages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | Check, For builders | `/demo`, `/product/case-file`, `/product/byok` |
| Use cases | Websites, Payees | `/use-cases/investment`, `clone-shops`, `impersonation`, `crypto-payees`, `marketplace` |
| Trust & Safety | The bounds | `/security`, `/security#flow`, `/security#disclosure` |
| Playground | Side room | `/world`, `/spot` |
| Developers | Learn, Code | `/how-it-works`, `/developers`, GitHub |

Sign in is a ghost button. Check now is the coral button and goes to `/demo`. On a small screen the same groups sit in a drawer; Check now stays in the bar.

The open panel is a white fill at 98%, with a strong blur, a hairline border, and a soft shadow, so the page cannot read through an item. A light plum scrim covers the page under the bar while a menu or the drawer is open. The same fill is on the mobile drawer.

Scout World and Spot the Scammer are not in the top-level bar. They appear under Playground, on `/playground`, and in the footer column.

## What we do not take

No Pricing menu. There is no plan to sell on this site. No Company menu, because there is no company. The badge is a coral-to-iris lantern, not a black square with an N.
