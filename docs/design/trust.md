# Trust and safety

The live pages are described in `trust-page.md`. This note is the earlier pass.

Trust here is a change of surface and a plain account of the bounds. It is not a wall of seals.

## References

- [RibbonPay security](https://ribbonpay.example/security). A designed page. Controls are named. The tone is specific.
- [Nebius](https://nebius.com). The “built by builders” band switches to a dark field so trust is a pause in the rhythm, with a few short claims rather than a paragraph.
- [Vercel security](https://vercel.com/security). Plain language, concrete controls, no trophy case.
- [MinnowRisk](https://www.minnowrisk.example). A risk product is expected to say what it will not do.
- [OWASP](https://owasp.org), [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) and [HMA FEAT](https://www.hma.example/publications/monographs-or-information-paper/2022/principles-to-promote-fairness-ethics-accountability-and-transparency-in-the-use-of-ai). We already map real controls to these. The page must not look like a certificate.

## What we take

Homepage: a plum band, white type, three true sentences (advice, labelled sources, pages treated as data), and a link to the control map. The open-source line sits here too, so the band has a human ending.

`/security` keeps the ten practices, the flow, and the residual-risk list. Numbers pick up iris. Flow cards sit on the four washes. The advisory stamp section keeps its id so the menu can land on it. No badges were added.

## What we do not take

No shield wallpaper, no “bank-grade” claim, no compliance logos, no invented audit firm.
