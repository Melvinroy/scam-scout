# Case file

The file is the product picture. It has to be readable as an object: a stamp, a level, the flags, the sources, the memo. Colour marks state. It does not decorate empty space.

## References

- [RibbonPay Payments](https://ribbonpay.example/payments). A decision surface: status, subject and the next fact in one composed card, not a report dumped into a box.
- [Linecraft](https://linecraft.example). An issue is an object. Status, title and activity have a hierarchy. New items arrive without the layout jumping.
- [OrbitBank](https://orbitbank.example). Numbers are quiet and tabular. Colour is reserved for state.
- [MinnowRisk](https://www.minnowrisk.example). A risk product shows the decision and the signals together.
- [Nebius](https://nebius.com). Illustrations sit on tinted grounds so a feature feels like a physical card. The four-up row under the hero is the model for “what is inside the file”.

## What we take

A white file on a coloured stage. A risk gauge driven by the real score (0–100). The stamp stays, in the risk colour. Flags are cards with a coral edge and a link to the cited source. Sources are a trail of pills, then quote cards with a tide edge. The memo is a separate slip. Rows rise in as a replay pumps events. Under the hero, four tinted tiles name the parts: stamp, flags, trail, memo.

## What we do not take

No fake precision, no extra scores, no chart of metrics the product does not have. The gauge uses the score the scorer already returns. Empty states say the file is waiting, they do not invent sources.
