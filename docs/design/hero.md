# Hero and live check

The first screen has to look like a product you can use, on a white field, with colour doing real work. The previous page was a black headline and a grey card. That is the failure this section is answering.

## References

- [Nebius](https://nebius.com). Captured 21 Oct 2026. White canvas, sticky bar, square logo badge, enormous headline, then a row of feature cards on blue, green, purple and orange fields. Each card is mostly a custom illustration. The acid-lime note is a highlight, not the whole page. Compact pill actions sit in the bar.
- [Nebius Token Factory](https://tokenfactory.nebius.com) and the [service page](https://nebius.com/services/token-factory). One promise, then product cards (a name and a single line) and grouped choices. A headless capture of the token host did not finish here; the page structure is taken from the live copy.
- [RibbonPay](https://ribbonpay.example). The thing you came to do is drawn in the first screen. Colour is a brand surface. One action is obvious.
- [Linecraft](https://linecraft.example). Tight type, a real product surface above the fold, motion that shows state.
- [SpendQuay](https://spendquay.example). White fintech page, saturated product cards, one primary action, short trust copy.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com). Very large type, one electric accent, the product framed rather than dropped on the grid.

## What we take

White background. A headline large enough to be the layout. The check itself in that first screen: input, primary button, example chips. The case file on a coloured stage beside it, so the page is showing the product, not describing it. A coral pill for the only primary action. Iris, tide and amber show up as washes and as the file, not as tiny dots.

## What we do not take

No lime wave, no navy wordmark, no borrowed illustrations, no Nebius sentences. The accent word “before you pay” is coral in our own type (Outfit), not a copy of their display.

## This section

- Headline is the existing promise, with the last phrase in coral.
- The check bar is a white pill on the page. Known examples play in the file. Anything else goes to sign-in or `/demo`, as before.
- Chips select a recorded case. The selected chip is iris, so coral stays reserved for Check.
- The file sits in a mesh of coral, iris and tide, with a slow drift. Reduced motion turns the drift off.
- The card is the scorecard in `case-file-evidence-sources.md`: an advisory gauge, six signal tiles, and a Full memo button. The map and the memo open in the drawer. The card does not scroll. The two chips are recorded public cases, not a live check.

How it works follows this section, then Examples, the trust band, a closing check, and the footer.
