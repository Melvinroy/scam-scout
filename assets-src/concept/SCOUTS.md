# Scouts — concept note

Date: 21 October 2026

The first pass was an original lantern-hood: a bean body, a warm point of light, a satchel, dot eyes. Not Minions, not Pikmin, not Among Us. That note still describes the jobs.

The shipped Scout World uses rigged CC0 adventurers from KayKit Character Pack: Adventures 1.0 by Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.com), with the city from KayKit City Builder Bits 1.0. Both are Creative Commons Zero. Weapons that shipped in the character pack are removed. An evidence card is parented to the right hand in the scene. We did not model the characters.

Jobs, not brands: seeker, reader, cartographer, inspector, lens, archivist, plus dispatcher, clerk and chief at the desk.
